# Concise & High-Impact Response Rule

- **Be Ultra-Compact**: Keep all responses short, direct, and high-signal. Avoid long paragraphs, repetitive intros, or filler text.
- **Short Sentences & Bullet Points**: Use brief, punchy bullet points (1–2 short sentences max per point) that remain technical, clear, and well-detailed.
- **Plan Format**:
  1. **Root Cause / Goal** (1–2 lines)
  2. **Action Plan** (2–4 concise bullets)
  3. **Prompt to Proceed** (1 line)
